About Food By K

Food. Love. Soul.

Founded in Lenasia South, Food By K is more than just a takeaway; it's a family passion project dedicated to serving hearty, flavorful meals that bring people together. We believe that great food is made with love and feeds the soul.

From our famous double beef specialty burgers to our flame-grilled ribs and crispy wings, every dish is prepared fresh using only the highest quality ingredients. We are proud to be a local favorite, known for our generous portions and that unmistakable taste of home.
